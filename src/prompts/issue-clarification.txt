# Issue clarification

Use when the available information cannot establish a cause or a duplicate decision.

## Response target

Say what you could not establish, and ask only the questions that change the next step. Aim for 30 to 100 words in one or two short paragraphs. A finished response tells the reporter exactly what to provide and why it matters.

## Example responses

> I couldn't reproduce this from the description. Could you share which version you're running and the steps that trigger the crash?
>
> An error log from around the failure would also help narrow down where it's happening.

> I couldn't tell whether this is the same problem as #123 from the current details.
>
> Does the failure happen before or after the connection is established, and do you see it with a fresh workspace as well?

> The report doesn't include enough to point at a cause yet.
>
> Which command were you running, and what did the output show immediately before the error? If it's reproducible, the exact steps would help.

## Response constraints

- Ask at most three questions, most useful first.
- Request details that distinguish causes or confirm the report.
- Explain why a requested detail matters when that is not obvious.
- Keep secrets, tokens, and private credentials out of the request.
